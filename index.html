About VitalMesh

VitalMesh is a live, AI-assisted health monitoring companion for a single patient.

It watches heart rate, oxygen, and temperature against this patient's own normal ranges, and only raises an alert after a condition genuinely persists — so real concerns are never missed, but momentary blips never cause panic.

Built for caregivers and families who can't watch every second, VitalMesh watches so you don't have to.

Built by a first-time hackathon team for HackFest'26.
